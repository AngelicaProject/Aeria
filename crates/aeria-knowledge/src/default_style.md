# Style

The decisions of this project's translators. Machine translation follows this
file over its own defaults; change anything here that the project decides
otherwise.

## Address

- Formal and informal address, toward the player character and between
  characters, follows the French localization: where it has "tu", the address
  is informal, and where it has "vous", formal. When the French line does not
  show it, a character keeps the address of their nearby lines.
- Quest journals, objectives, tutorials, and system messages address the
  player formally.

## Names

- Personal names are not translated but written in the target language's
  script by their sound: Japanese names by the usual transcription of Japanese
  in the target language, other names by how they sound in the English
  localization. Nicknames and titles with a meaning are translated.
- In a place name, the proper part is written by sound and the descriptive
  part is translated: in "Western Thanalan", "Western" is translated and
  "Thanalan" is not.
- Names of monsters, items, actions, and statuses are translated by meaning
  and are as short as the original. Tiers stay Roman numerals.

## Interface

- Buttons, menu items, and labels are no longer than the English where the
  language allows, with only abbreviations common in the target language.

## Writing

- The target language's own quotation marks, dashes, and number formats.
